# Overview of the iMall Platform

_Last updated: 2026-02-13_

iMall (`imall.ge`) is an **online marketplace** that enables third-party vendors to offer their products and services to customers. The platform does **not** sell its own products; instead, it acts as a **mediator** connecting vendors and buyers.

The marketplace provides tools for listing items, communicating with buyers, and facilitating transactions, including the option to host auctions for permitted products.
