# Cookies Policy

_Last updated: 2026-02-13_

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember session state, preferences, and security context.

They help us keep you signed in, keep carts stable, and improve navigation quality.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential cookies:** required for authentication, checkout, and platform security.
- **Analytics cookies:** help us understand usage patterns and improve performance.
- **Preference cookies:** remember your language and interface preferences.

## 3. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling non-essential cookies may reduce personalization.

Disabling essential cookies may affect core platform functionality.
