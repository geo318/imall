# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-02-13_

iMall collects, uses, and protects personal data so the marketplace can function securely and reliably. By creating an account or using the platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, shipping details, and order information.

We may also collect technical and usage data, including IP address, browser, device type, and interaction events to improve platform performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to process transactions, manage accounts, personalize your experience, and communicate updates about orders, returns, and account activity.

Where legally permitted, we may also use your data for fraud prevention, security monitoring, and service improvement.

## 3. Data Security

We apply administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information.

Payment processing is handled by third-party providers, and iMall does not store full card numbers.

## 4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To submit a privacy request, contact **contact@imall.ge**.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with trusted partners such as payment processors, hosting providers, analytics tools, and logistics services to operate the marketplace.

These partners are required to protect your data and process it only for authorized purposes.
